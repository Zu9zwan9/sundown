# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

> **Read before using.** I am not a lawyer and this is not legal advice. This
> is a conventional mutual NDA template assembled from standard commercial
> practice. Have a qualified attorney in your jurisdiction review it before
> you sign it or send it to anyone. Governing law, enforceability of
> restrictive terms, and trade-secret handling vary significantly by state and
> country — the bracketed choices below are not neutral defaults.
>
> Fill every `[BRACKETED]` field. An unfilled bracket in a signed agreement is
> a real problem, not a typo.

---

## What to decide before you send it

Five clauses do most of the work. Decide them deliberately rather than
accepting whatever the other side's template says.

| Clause | Common range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §6 Term of confidentiality | 2–5 years | 3 is the usual landing point. Trade secrets are carved out to survive indefinitely (§6.2) — keep that carve-out. |
| §2.2 Marking requirement | Usually omit | Requiring every oral disclosure to be confirmed in writing within 30 days sounds tidy and is routinely forgotten, which silently un-protects the disclosure. |
| §8 Residuals | Often deleted | A residuals clause lets the other side use whatever their people remember. Standard for large software companies, dangerous for a small one disclosing a novel design. Ours is bracketed as optional and off by default. |
| §12 Governing law | Your jurisdiction | The party that concedes venue concedes real leverage. Expect this to be negotiated. |
| Non-solicitation | Not present | Deliberately absent. Non-solicits are frequently smuggled into NDAs. If the other side adds one, that is a separate commercial negotiation, not boilerplate. |

## MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is entered into
and effective as of **[EFFECTIVE DATE]** (the **"Effective Date"**) by and
between:

**[PARTY A LEGAL NAME]**, a [ENTITY TYPE, e.g. Delaware corporation] with its
principal place of business at [PARTY A ADDRESS] (**"Party A"**); and

**[PARTY B LEGAL NAME]**, a [ENTITY TYPE] with its principal place of business
at [PARTY B ADDRESS] (**"Party B"**).

Party A and Party B are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties."**
In relation to any particular disclosure, the Party disclosing information is
the **"Discloser"** and the Party receiving it is the **"Recipient."**

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore **[DESCRIBE THE PURPOSE — e.g. "a potential
commercial relationship concerning developer tooling software"]** (the
**"Purpose"**). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose to the
other certain confidential and proprietary information. This Agreement governs
that disclosure.

### 2. Confidential Information

**2.1** **"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information
disclosed by a Discloser to a Recipient, in any form, relating to the
Discloser's business, products, or affairs — including, without limitation,
source code, object code, algorithms, architectures, technical
specifications, designs, research, product plans and roadmaps, unreleased
features, pricing, financial information, business plans, forecasts, customer
and supplier lists, personnel information, and the terms of any prospective
agreement between the Parties.

**2.2** Confidential Information includes information that a reasonable person
in the Recipient's position would understand to be confidential given its
nature and the circumstances of disclosure, whether or not it is marked or
identified as confidential.

**2.3** The existence and terms of this Agreement, and the fact that the
Parties are discussing the Purpose, are each Confidential Information of both
Parties.

### 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can
demonstrate by contemporaneous written records:

**(a)** was rightfully in the Recipient's possession, without restriction,
before the Discloser disclosed it;

**(b)** is or becomes publicly available through no act or omission of the
Recipient or anyone acting on its behalf;

**(c)** is rightfully received by the Recipient from a third party who is not
under any obligation of confidentiality to the Discloser; or

**(d)** is independently developed by the Recipient's personnel who had no
access to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

### 4. Obligations of the Recipient

**4.1 Use.** The Recipient will use the Discloser's Confidential Information
solely for the Purpose, and for no other purpose.

**4.2 Non-disclosure.** The Recipient will not disclose the Discloser's
Confidential Information to any third party except as expressly permitted by
this Agreement.

**4.3 Standard of care.** The Recipient will protect the Discloser's
Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses for its own
confidential information of similar importance, and in no event less than a
reasonable degree of care.

**4.4 Permitted recipients.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential
Information only to those of its employees, officers, directors, and
professional advisors who (a) have a genuine need to know it for the Purpose
and (b) are bound by written or professional obligations of confidentiality at
least as protective as this Agreement. The Recipient remains responsible for
any breach by such persons as though it were the Recipient's own.

**4.5 Security incidents.** The Recipient will notify the Discloser promptly
in writing on becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure of the
Discloser's Confidential Information, and will cooperate reasonably with the
Discloser to limit the consequences.

### 5. Compelled disclosure

If the Recipient is required by law, regulation, subpoena, or court order to
disclose any Confidential Information, it may do so, provided that — to the
extent legally permitted — it gives the Discloser prompt written notice so the
Discloser may seek a protective order, discloses only the portion legally
required, and uses reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment for the
disclosed material.

### 6. Term and survival

**6.1** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for
**[TERM — e.g. two (2) years]**, unless earlier terminated by either Party on
**[NOTICE PERIOD — e.g. thirty (30) days']** prior written notice.

**6.2** The obligations in Sections 4 and 5 survive expiry or termination and
continue for **[SURVIVAL PERIOD — e.g. three (3) years]** from the date of
each disclosure. Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret
under applicable law remains protected for as long as it qualifies as a trade
secret, without time limit.

### 7. Return or destruction

On the Discloser's written request, or on expiry or termination of this
Agreement, the Recipient will promptly return or destroy all Confidential
Information of the Discloser in its possession and, if asked, certify in
writing that it has done so. The Recipient may retain (a) one archival copy
solely to evidence compliance with this Agreement and (b) copies held in
routine, non-targeted automated backups — in each case subject to the
continuing obligations of this Agreement for as long as they are retained.

### 8. Residuals — *optional, deleted by default*

> **Include this clause only deliberately.** It permits the Recipient's people
> to use unaided memory of the Discloser's information for any purpose, which
> substantially weakens the protection. Standard in large-company templates,
> unfavourable to the party disclosing something novel.

*[OPTIONAL] Nothing in this Agreement restricts the Recipient's personnel from
using, in the ordinary course of the Recipient's business, general knowledge,
skills, and experience — including ideas, concepts, and techniques retained in
unaided memory — acquired during the performance of this Agreement, provided
that this does not authorise disclosure of the Discloser's Confidential
Information or use of any of the Discloser's intellectual property rights.*

### 9. No license, no obligation, no warranty

**9.1** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser.
Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any licence or right, express
or implied, under any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other
intellectual property right.

**9.2** Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to disclose any
information, to proceed with the Purpose, or to enter into any further
agreement. Either Party may terminate discussions at any time.

**9.3** Confidential Information is provided **"as is."** Neither Party makes
any representation or warranty as to its accuracy or completeness, and neither
Party will be liable for the other's reliance on it.

### 10. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential
Information may cause harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate
remedy. Accordingly, the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive or other
equitable relief, without the necessity of posting a bond, in addition to any
other remedies available at law or in equity.

### 11. No publicity

Neither Party will use the other's name, logo, or trademarks, or issue any
press release or public statement referring to this Agreement or the Purpose,
without the other Party's prior written consent.

### 12. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **[GOVERNING JURISDICTION — e.g. the
State of Delaware, USA]**, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The
Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in
**[VENUE]** for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

### 13. General

**13.1 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the
other's prior written consent, except to a successor in connection with a
merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets, on written
notice to the other Party.

**13.2 Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the
Parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and
agreements relating to it.

**13.3 Amendment and waiver.** Any amendment must be in writing and signed by
both Parties. A failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver of it.

**13.4 Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, it will be
modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the
remaining provisions will continue in full force.

**13.5 Notices.** Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above
(or to an address later designated in writing), and are effective on personal
delivery, on confirmed email delivery, or three (3) business days after
dispatch by recognised courier.

**13.6 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts,
including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of
which together constitute one instrument.

**13.7 Relationship.** Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint
venture, agency, or employment relationship between the Parties.
